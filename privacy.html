Privacy Policy

Last updated 30 October 2026

Gloopsy is a puzzle game for iPhone made by Jacob Costolo.

We don't collect any data

Gloopsy has no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking, and it never connects to the internet. Nothing you do in the game is sent to us or to anyone else.

What stays on your iPhone

The game saves your level progress on your iPhone: which levels you've finished, plus your best stars and move counts. It uses standard iOS storage, never leaves your device, and we can't see it. Deleting the app deletes it.

Permissions

Gloopsy doesn't use your location, contacts, photos, camera, microphone or any other personal information.

Children

Gloopsy is suitable for all ages and collects nothing from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
