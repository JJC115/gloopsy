Gloopsy

Tilt. Slide. Squish.

How to play

  • Swipe to tilt the board. Every jelly slides until it bumps into something.
  • Same colours squish together when one slides into another.
  • Squish each colour down to one jelly to win. Match par for 3 stars.
  • Grey stones slide too, but they never squish.
  • Honey pads stop anything that slides onto them.

Questions

I'm stuck on a level

Undo and restart are free and unlimited. The light-bulb button shows you the next move. Using it caps that try at 2 stars. If the jellies look worried, there's no way to win from where they are, so undo a move or two.

Where is my progress saved?

Only on your iPhone. Gloopsy has no accounts and never goes online, so deleting the app also deletes your progress.

Are there ads or in-app purchases?

No. No ads, no purchases, no tracking.

Does it need an internet connection?

No. Gloopsy works fully offline.

Which devices does it run on?

iPhone with iOS 17 or later.
